## Project context

This McGill Engine project supported the biotechnology startup VVector Bio. The work focused on translating a technical product concept into a credible business plan and venture-capital pitch.

## Research and analysis

I researched market size, industry dynamics, competition, product penetration opportunities, and financial considerations. Surveys, stakeholder conversations, and biotechnology research helped identify customer pain points and inform a product cost-benefit analysis.

## Operating plan

The project combined the research into an operating plan, budget, and future marketing direction. YouTrack supported project coordination, while LaTeX was used to document and present the work.

## Limitations

The project reflects an early-stage planning exercise rather than measured commercial performance. Any future expansion should separate research assumptions from results achieved after the engagement.
